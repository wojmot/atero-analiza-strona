Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the atero_analiza project (the "Project") collects, why, and how it is handled. The Project is private, non-commercial and run by an individual for personal portfolio and educational purposes.

1. Data we collect

Only with the account owner's explicit consent, the Project collects:

  • TikTok (via Login Kit / Display API, scopes user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list): basic profile information (e.g. display name, avatar, open ID), profile statistics (e.g. follower, following, likes and video counts) and a list of public videos with their statistics (e.g. views, likes, comments, shares) and metadata (e.g. title, description, creation date).
  • YouTube: channel information and statistics of channel videos (e.g. views, likes, comments).
  • Spotify: artist profile information and statistics (e.g. followers, popularity, tracks and releases).

The Project does not collect passwords, private messages, contact lists, payment data or any data beyond the permissions granted.

2. Purpose

The data is used solely for data analysis within a private portfolio and educational project (e.g. analysing reach and engagement over time). It is not used for advertising, profiling for marketing, or any commercial purpose.

3. Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with any third parties. Access to the data is limited to the Project's author and the person the data relates to.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored privately by the author and protected with reasonable measures. Access tokens are kept confidential and used only to retrieve the data described above. Data is kept only as long as needed for the Project, and is deleted when the Project ends or on request.

5. Your rights and withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent and revoke the Project's access at any time in the settings of your own TikTok, YouTube or Spotify account (in the section listing connected or authorised apps). You may also contact the author to request access to, correction of or deletion of your data; such requests will be handled promptly.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update shown above.
